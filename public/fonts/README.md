The unmodified Latin WOFF2 fonts are bundled under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
Each family's copyright notice and licence are included alongside the font files.

- Bricolage Grotesque: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/bricolagegrotesque
- DM Sans: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmsans
- Instrument Serif: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/instrumentserif

Mixtape's covers and parcel graphics are original typography and geometric artwork.
They are not official film posters, book covers, album artwork or artist portraits.

`bricolage-og.ttf` is the static 600-weight Bricolage Grotesque font from Google Fonts, used by the server-side Open Graph renderer. Same bundled OFL licence. Source: https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Bricolage+Grotesque:600

`instrument-serif-italic-og.ttf` and `dm-sans-og.ttf` are the bundled Instrument Serif Italic and DM Sans WOFF2 files above, unpacked to TrueType (DM Sans pinned to its 400 instance) because the image renderer cannot read WOFF2. They are used only for the homepage share card and the repository's social preview. Same OFL licences.
